MAJESTY: THE FANTASY KINGDOM SIM - COMBAT SYSTEM
=================================================

OVERVIEW
========
Combat in Majesty follows a specific sequence: chance to hit, damage calculation, and damage reduction. The system uses percentage rolls (1-100) to determine outcomes.


PHASE 1: CHANCE TO HIT
======================

MELEE ATTACKS (vs Heroes/Monsters)
----------------------------------
1. Attacker makes a percentage roll against their Hand-to-Hand ability
   - If roll ≤ Hand-to-Hand: Attack proceeds
   - If roll > Hand-to-Hand: Attack misses

2. If attack proceeds, Defender makes a percentage roll against their Parry ability
   - If roll ≤ Parry: Attack is completely avoided (no damage)
   - If roll > Parry: Attack hits (proceed to Phase 2)

EXAMPLE: Warrior (Hand-to-Hand 70) attacking a Goblin
- Roll 65: Hits (65 ≤ 70), Goblin rolls against Parry
- Goblin has Parry 40, rolls 35: Goblin avoids (35 ≤ 40)
- Result: No damage dealt


RANGED ATTACKS (vs Heroes/Monsters)
-----------------------------------
1. Attacker makes a percentage roll against their Ranged ability
   - If roll ≤ Ranged: Attack proceeds
   - If roll > Ranged: Attack misses

2. If attack proceeds, Defender makes a percentage roll against their Dodge ability
   - If roll ≤ Dodge: Attack is completely avoided (no damage)
   - If roll > Dodge: Attack hits (proceed to Phase 2)

EXAMPLE: Ranger (Ranged 75) shooting at a Vampire
- Roll 60: Hits (60 ≤ 75), Vampire rolls against Dodge
- Vampire has Dodge 50, rolls 55: Hit gets through (55 > 50)
- Result: Proceed to damage calculation


ATTACKS VS LAIRS/BUILDINGS
---------------------------
Attacks on lairs and buildings automatically hit. No roll needed.


SPELL ATTACKS (vs Heroes/Monsters)
----------------------------------
Magical attacks automatically hit the target.

Defender makes a percentage roll against their Magic Resistance to completely avoid:
- If roll ≤ Magic Resistance: Spell is completely avoided (no damage)
- If roll > Magic Resistance: Spell hits (proceed to Phase 2)

EXAMPLE: Wizard casting Fire Blast on a Hell Bear
- Fire Blast automatically targets Hell Bear
- Hell Bear has Magic Resistance 30, rolls 45: Hit gets through (45 > 30)
- Result: Proceed to damage calculation


PHASE 2: INITIAL DAMAGE CALCULATION
====================================

MELEE/RANGED ATTACKS by HEROES
------------------------------
Damage = (1 to Weapon Damage) + (1 per magic bonus of weapon) + (Hero's Strength / 8)

Breaking this down:
- Random damage from weapon: 1 to [Weapon Damage stat]
- Bonus from magical enchantments on weapon: +1 per magic level
- Strength bonus: Strength divided by 8 (rounded down)

EXAMPLE: Warrior with Longsword attacking
- Warrior's Strength: 18
- Longsword Weapon Damage: 10
- Longsword has no magic bonus
- Damage = (1-10) + 0 + (18/8) = (1-10) + 2 = 3-12 damage


MELEE/RANGED ATTACKS by MONSTERS
--------------------------------
X = Monster's Strength / 4 (rounded down)
Y = Monster's Strength - X
Damage = (Random Number from 1 to Y) + X

EXAMPLE: Hell Bear with Strength 35 attacking
- X = 35 / 4 = 8
- Y = 35 - 8 = 27
- Damage = (Random 1-27) + 8 = 9-35 damage


SPELL ATTACKS by HEROES
-----------------------
Damage = Spell's Base Damage + (Caster's Intelligence / 4)

Breaking this down:
- Each spell has a base damage range (e.g., Fire Blast does 8-20)
- Add bonus from caster's Intelligence divided by 4 (rounded down)

EXAMPLE: Wizard with Intelligence 25 casting Fire Blast
- Fire Blast base damage: 8-20
- Intelligence bonus: 25 / 4 = 6
- Total damage: 14-26 damage


SPELL DAMAGE RANGES
------------------
When a spell does a range of damage (e.g., 16-35):
- Generate random number from 1 to maximum (1-35 in this case)
- If result is below minimum, use minimum damage (16)
- This means minimum damage is MORE likely than maximum

EXAMPLE: Meteor Storm with range 16-35
- Possible rolls: 1-35
- Rolls 1-15 become: 16 damage
- Rolls 16-35 become: 16-35 damage
- Result: More common to do lower damage


SPELL ATTACKS by MONSTERS
-------------------------
Monster spells follow similar rules to hero spells, with damage increased by (Monster's Intelligence / 4) if the monster has that stat.


PHASE 3: DAMAGE REDUCTION
==========================

DEFENDING AGAINST MELEE/RANGED ATTACKS
--------------------------------------
Armor reduces incoming physical damage.

Formula:
X = Armor / 2 (rounded down)
Y = Armor - X - 1
Z = (Random Number from 0 to Y) + (1 per magic bonus of armor)
Damage Stopped = X + Z

Breaking this down:
- Base armor reduction: Armor / 2
- Variable reduction: Random amount from 0 to (Armor - X - 1)
- Magical armor bonus: +1 per enchantment level
- These add together to reduce incoming damage

EXAMPLE: Paladin with Armor 10, Magic Plate Armor (magic bonus +1)
- X = 10 / 2 = 5
- Y = 10 - 5 - 1 = 4
- Z = (Random 0-4) + 1 = 1-5
- Damage Stopped = 5 + (1-5) = 6-10 damage reduced
- If attack would do 12 damage, Paladin takes 2-6 damage


DEFENDING AGAINST SPELL ATTACKS
--------------------------------
Magic Resistance first prevents spell (see Phase 1).

If spell hits, spell damage is reduced by:
- 2 points per point of armor enchantment

EXAMPLE: Wizard wearing armor with +3 magic bonus is hit by Fireball
- Fireball does 15 damage
- Armor magic bonus: 3 points
- Damage reduction: 3 × 2 = 6 points
- Wizard takes: 15 - 6 = 9 damage


DEFENDING BUILDINGS/LAIRS
--------------------------
All damage to buildings and lairs is automatically halved.

EXAMPLE: Ballista Tower fires at a Goblin Camp with 200 Hit Points
- Ballista does 20 damage
- Building damage reduction: 20 / 2 = 10 damage
- Goblin Camp takes 10 damage, now at 190 Hit Points


DAMAGE SUMMARY TABLE
====================

Attack Type          Accuracy Check              Bypass               Reduction
-----------          ---------------              ------               ---------
Melee (Hero)         Hand-to-Hand vs target       Parry                Armor
Ranged (Hero)        Ranged vs target            Dodge                Armor
Melee (Monster)      Automatic                   Parry                Armor
Ranged (Monster)     Automatic                   Dodge                Armor
Spell (Hero/Monster) Automatic                   Magic Resistance     Armor enchant × 2
Building/Lair        Automatic                   N/A                  50% reduction


CRITICAL HITS
=============
Some heroes can deal critical hits (Barbarians, Warriors of Discord):
- Critical Hit chance: Listed as percentage (e.g., 3%)
- Effect: Target instantly killed (or takes 1/6 of HP if has Resist Critical Hit)
- Defenders with Resist Critical Hit ability instead take 1/6 damage


COMBAT EXAMPLES
===============

EXAMPLE 1: Ranger vs Skeleton
Ranger: Hand-to-Hand None, Ranged 75, Dodge 50, Strength 12, Damage 6
Skeleton: Dodge 40, Armor 0

1. Chance to Hit:
   - Ranger rolls 65 vs Ranged 75: Hit (65 ≤ 75)
   - Skeleton rolls 35 vs Dodge 40: Hit gets through (35 > 40)

2. Damage Calculation:
   - (1-6) + (12/8) = (1-6) + 1 = 2-7 damage
   - Roll comes up 5: Total damage = 5 points

3. Damage Reduction:
   - Skeleton armor = 0
   - No reduction
   - Skeleton takes 5 damage


EXAMPLE 2: Wizard vs Hell Bear
Wizard: Spell attack (Fire Blast 8-20), Intelligence 25
Hell Bear: Magic Resistance 30, Armor 8

1. Chance to Hit:
   - Fire Blast automatically targets Hell Bear
   - Hell Bear rolls 35 vs Magic Resistance 30: Hit gets through (35 > 30)

2. Damage Calculation:
   - Fire Blast base: 8-20
   - Intelligence bonus: 25 / 4 = 6
   - Total damage range: 14-26
   - Roll comes up 18: Total damage = 18 points

3. Damage Reduction:
   - Armor 8 defending against spell
   - Magic bonus (if any): Let's say +2
   - Damage stopped: 2 × 2 = 4 points
   - Hell Bear takes: 18 - 4 = 14 damage


KEY COMBAT MECHANICS
====================

ACCURACY & DIFFICULTY
- Stats above 95 cap at 95 (defensive stats cap at 85 in multiplayer)
- Stats above cap provide minute benefits to tournament performance
- Combat becomes more reliable with higher stats

ARMOR EFFECTIVENESS
- Armor is more effective against multiple attacks than single large attacks
- Magical armor enchantments are crucial for spell resistance
- Buildings take 50% damage, making them harder to destroy

SPELL ADVANTAGES
- Spells automatically hit (bypassing accuracy rolls)
- Only Magic Resistance can prevent them
- Intelligence provides both spell damage and survivability

MONSTER COMBAT
- Monster attacks automatically hit (no accuracy check)
- Defenders still get Parry/Dodge/Magic Resistance rolls
- Monster damage scales with their Strength stat

LEVELING BENEFITS
- Most combat stats increase by 1 per level
- Key attributes increase by 1 every other level
- Level matters significantly in combat outcomes
