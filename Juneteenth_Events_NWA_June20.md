# Juneteenth Events in Northwest Arkansas - June 20, 2026

## Fayetteville
### 30th Annual NWA Juneteenth Celebration
- **Time:** 11:00 AM – 3:00 PM
- **Location:** The Upper Ramble, 255 S. West Avenue, Fayetteville (between Dickson & Center Street)
- **Details:** Free and open to the public. The event will feature live music, cultural performances, children's activities, games, giveaways, a vendor market, a live DJ, and a musical headliner. Local food and community resource tables will also be provided. No registration required — just show up with your whole crew! Organized by the University of Arkansas and the NWA Juneteenth Planning Committee to commemorate 30 years of this tradition.

## Springdale
### 5th Annual Freedom Festival
- **Time:** 3:00 PM – 10:00 PM
- **Location:** Luther George Park, Springdale
- **Details:** Large, family-friendly festival with activities for kids (games, activities), vendor market (local businesses), horse riding, giveaways, and a food truck market. Enjoy live performances by Hurricane Chris, Rose Royce, and GS Boyz. This festival celebrates the richness of Black musical traditions and aims to foster a unified community.

---

Both events are among the largest Juneteenth celebrations in Arkansas and offer a vibrant atmosphere with music, culture, food, and community engagement.
