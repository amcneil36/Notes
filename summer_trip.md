## Eureka Springs

1. **Blue Spring Heritage Center** - Natural springs and water activities (Open: 9am-6pm Sat/Sun)
   - Address: 1537 Co Rd 210, Eureka Springs, AR 72632

2. **Thorncrown Chapel** - Stunning glass chapel nestled in the woods
   - Address: 12968 US-62, Eureka Springs, AR 72632

3. **Eureka Springs downtown** - Historic downtown area with shops, galleries, and restaurants
   - Address: Basin Spring Park, 4 Spring St, Eureka Springs, AR 72632

4. **The Grand Central Hotel** - Historic hotel with shops and restaurants
   - Address: 37 N Main St, Eureka Springs, AR 72632

5. **Eureka Springs music park** - Music and entertainment venue
   - Address: Basin Spring Park, 4 Spring St, Eureka Springs, AR 72632

6. **The Crescent Hotel** - Historic "haunted" hotel with tours available
   - Address: 75 Prospect Ave, Eureka Springs, AR 72632

7. **Onyx Cave Park** - Natural cave formations you can explore (Open: 10am-4pm Sundays, 9am-5pm Saturdays)
   - Address: 338 Onyx Cave Lane, Eureka Springs, AR 72632
